# Crimocracy Foundation Status

This file records the architectural foundation currently present in the repository. It is not a feature roadmap and does not override `AGENTS.md` or `GAME_DESIGN.md`.

## Verification Surface

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` is the single cached verification job for pushes and pull requests. It
  checks formatting, all-target compilation, Clippy, the unit/doc-test suite, and the targeted smoke
  harness test. Superseded runs on the same branch are cancelled; release builds and long-form
  gameplay reports remain deliberate local checks rather than part of every change gate.
- `examples/gameplay_harness.rs` has explicit `smoke` and `full` modes. Smoke exercises RUSH, PRESS,
  and RECON through terminal state plus the legal-foundation path and validates per-run terminal,
  player-knowledge, standing-contingency, decision, surveillance, and legal-consequence contracts.
  Full mode retains the narrative, matched-seed batch, property liquidation, legal foundation, and
  bounded scenario-sensitivity evidence. Narrative sessions observe two simulated days; batch runs
  observe one day so repeated routine ticks do not dominate the evidence or runtime. `--seed`
  selects the matched simulation seed and `--samples` remains bounded to 1..=64.
- Harness CLI parsing has focused tests, and the smoke contract is run as an example test so CI
  reuses one compiled target instead of launching a second untracked verification path.

## Current Foundation

- Deterministic, serializable `AppState` owns all mutable campaign state and persisted domain-separated RNG streams. Generic, operation, investigation, legitimate-business, and enterprise randomness advance independently, so extra work in one simulation domain cannot perturb unrelated outcomes while save/load still preserves exact deterministic continuation.
- Persistent references use typed `u32` IDs allocated by state-owned counters.
- Immutable authored definitions are assembled by the startup `Registry` from Rust content builders.
- World records cover organizations, characters, neighborhoods, and businesses with synchronized lookup indexes. Neighborhoods carry explicit economic and institutional profiles for wealth, commercial activity, illicit demand, police presence, political influence, social cohesion, and tolerance for visible violence. Businesses carry authored kinds plus concrete organizational functions such as cash intensity, vehicle access, warehousing, meeting space, customer access, union access, distribution infrastructure, and professional records rather than percentage-bonus upgrades.
- Character organization membership and supervision changes use versioned validated reassignment tokens. Commit revalidates the current hierarchy and active dependencies, so later supervisor, mandate, operation, investigation, custody, or direct-report changes cannot make an older token bypass organization-integrity rules. Organization changes require direct reports to be reassigned first and are rejected while the character owns active operational, delegated, or investigative responsibility or is actively detained.
- Directional relationships are persistent records with bounded dimensions and a single mutation system. Social state maintains both source and target indexes, so relational systems can traverse incoming as well as outgoing ties without scanning the world roster.
- Character drives are authored registry-backed definitions rather than ad hoc runtime keys. The current design set includes money, status, safety, respect, revenge, family security, political advancement, independence, and ideological cause; character creation validates every stored drive against the loaded content registry.
- Intelligence is stored as provenance-bearing records with holder, source, typed topic, subject, observation time, reliability, specificity, and explicit source-information lineage. Typed topics include target security, personnel, schedules, police activity, routes, relationships, financial performance, legal activity, market access, and operational outcomes. Holder/topic indexes support focused retrieval without scanning all knowledge. Original information cannot author lineage or masquerade as an internal report; only the canonical versioned transfer path can create an `InternalReport`, with exactly one source-information record, a source entity matching that source holder, and the same topic as its source. Transfers are tied to current organizational relationships, transferred records remain organization-held historical knowledge after the reporting character later leaves, and reverse lineage indexes support traceability from source information to derived internal reports.
- Operation opportunities are persistent provenance-backed situations rather than random mission cards.
  - Discovery: requires an active criminal organization plus organization-held information recorded no later than discovery that directly covers every target entity; character-held knowledge and another organization's knowledge cannot be used implicitly. Discovery atomically creates a `Notable` `Opportunity` report citing the exact source-information IDs and target entities.
  - Indexes: opportunity state owns synchronized indexes by organization, target entity, source information, discovery/lifecycle report, exact open organization/kind/target context, expiry time, and converted operation, so provenance can be traversed in both directions without scans while duplicate open cards for the same situation are prevented without blocking later rediscovery after dismissal or expiry.
  - Lifecycle: open opportunities may be dismissed, expire deterministically through the minute tick at their semantic deadline, or convert only through a versioned token into an `Authorized` operation with the same organization, authored operation kind, and exact objective target set. Lifecycle commits remove open-context and expiry-index membership unconditionally before debug-only consistency checks, so optimized builds preserve the same index semantics as debug builds.
  - Expiry: automatic expiry is a multi-resource transaction that atomically records the terminal lifecycle and a `Notable` opportunity report carrying the original information provenance and targets; this prevents previously briefed opportunities from disappearing silently after their discovery report leaves the executive-brief source window. Player dismissal remains intentionally silent.
  - History: converted, dismissed, and expired records remain historical and survive save/load. Executive synthesis suppresses the original discovery report once an opportunity is no longer open while still allowing later terminal lifecycle reports such as expiry to surface, preventing converted opportunities from reappearing as stale current work.
- Semantic operations model objective, approach, leader, roles, explicitly selected planning intelligence, constraints, contingencies, schedule, execution timing, and lifecycle. Authorization uses a consuming validated token that snapshots leader and role-participant versions, requires active participants, rejects commits after dependent personnel change or the scheduled authorization time has passed, and accepts intelligence only when it is already held by the responsible organization and relevant to the authored operation plan.
- Operation execution profiles are immutable registry definitions grouped into difficulty, intelligence, and exposure profiles. Authored difficulty definitions provide duration, base difficulty, per-role capability mapping, per-approach difficulty adjustment, police-pressure weight, bounded variance, and objective-outcome thresholds. Intelligence definitions specify relevant topics, maximum useful age, and maximum execution advantage. Runtime intelligence quality is derived from the best selected report per relevant topic using reliability, specificity, and freshness; missing topics contribute no knowledge and duplicate reports do not stack. Persisted resolution factors also record covered versus relevant topic counts, allowing after-action reports to explain partial-but-useful intelligence coverage without relabeling a coverage-weighted mechanical score as a qualitative judgment. Runtime resolution consumes assigned-role capability, leader Management capability, selected planning intelligence, modeled target police presence, deadline pressure, and the operation-domain RNG stream rather than a universal success percentage.
- Authorized operations whose scheduled time arrives begin through the deterministic top-level tick pipeline in stable ID order and are indexed by due resolution time rather than scanned every tick.
  - Resolution: due operations resolve in stable order to `Achieved`, `Partial`, or `Failed` objective outcomes with persisted causal factors.
  - Exposure: a separate deterministic exposure calculation uses authored operation exposure, local police presence, participant Stealth capability, selected planning intelligence, approach, and its own state-owned RNG draw, so objective success and operational exposure are not the same result. Exposure persists as `None`, `Trace`, `Witnessed`, or `Identifying` with its causal factors.
  - Patrol pressure: patrol presence used for execution and exposure pressure is time-weighted across the actual operation interval rather than sampled only at the resolution instant; explicit patrol schedules remain bounded to a reusable daily profile and snapshot all relevant deployment versions. Police dispatch still evaluates the operation's alert context at its actual start time.
  - Completion: atomically creates organization-held `AfterAction` information, a concise `AfterAction` report, and a linked campaign-history event, and the operation stores all three durable record IDs rather than caching an isolated result string.
  - Aborts: every abort persists its exact time, phase, and cause. An in-progress leadership abort or decision-driven abort creates organization-held operational-outcome information, a synchronized after-action report, and campaign history; a pre-start cancellation records only the terminal cause and does not fabricate execution artifacts. Decision-driven aborts persist the exact authorizing decision ID and resolution timestamp.
  - Validation: abort validation snapshots operation version, lifecycle state, time, and pending-decision provenance before any mutation, and release-safe validation enforces unique after-action information/report/history links for both completed and started-aborted operations. An exposed operation now gives its organization limited `LegalActivity` knowledge when a jurisdictional case is opened: case existence and authority are surfaced, while evidence, lead, and detective work remain hidden.
- Authority decisions are durable typed records with requester, recipient, context, attention class, context-specific responses, timestamps, lifecycle, and version. Decision state owns synchronized pending indexes by recipient and typed context rather than assuming every request belongs to an operation. Operation exceptions retain `Continue`/`Abort` semantics: an operation in `AwaitingDecision` has exactly one pending decision and can leave that state only through decision resolution. Waiting time is excluded from execution time: the operation records its pause point, leaves the due-work index while awaiting input, and shifts its resolution deadline by the exact paused duration if ordered to continue. An `Abort` response carries a validated operation-abort transaction, so the abort's causal artifacts are committed while the request is still the operation's exact pending decision and the final operation abort record points back to that same resolved decision. Personnel recruitment approvals use a separate typed context and `Approve`/`Reject` response contract without overloading operation semantics.
- Attention classes are shared simulation data (`Routine`, `Notable`, `Exception`, `Crisis`). Persistent campaign settings control which classes request adapter-level auto-pause behavior.
- Manager delegation is represented by persistent mandates with organization ownership, one active mandate per manager, geographic and functional responsibility scopes, standing policy overrides, optional budget authority, versioning, historical revocation, and synchronized indexes.
- Delegated authority has one canonical resolution path that validates mandate lifecycle, manager ownership and membership, responsibility scope, and versioned manager/mandate dependencies. Finance and routine enterprises consume the same authority model. Active enterprises prevent revoking their mandate or removing the scope they currently depend on; suspension releases that active dependency and resumption requires fresh authority.
- Manager policy resolution is deterministic: an active mandate standing order overrides the organization policy for that manager; otherwise the organization policy is inherited.
- Recruitment and criminal-organization defection are persistent relational decisions rather than a global hiring list or a loyalty percentage. Candidate discovery follows concrete incoming relationship edges to a recruiter and reuses the canonical character-reassignment path, so active operations, mandates, investigations, informant-handler conflicts, and direct-report dependencies cannot be bypassed by personnel recruitment. A deterministic authored `RecruitmentDefinition` owns cooldowns, recruiter capabilities, drive mappings, relationship weighting, legal-pressure freshness/quality, factor weights, and conditional trait effects. Attempts persist their exact recruiter/incumbent relationship snapshots, former membership, selected candidate-held police-pressure information, causal factors, outcome, authority snapshot, and organization-held Personnel after-action information. Accepted attempts also create campaign history and atomically move the candidate under the recruiter; refusals preserve history without membership mutation. Repeated approaches to the same candidate/organization pair obey the authored cooldown instead of rerolling a hidden chance.
- Direct recruitment represents executive-approved action. Independent manager recruitment supports both authored approval paths while preserving one canonical recruitment outcome pipeline. Under `IndependentRecruitment = RequireApproval`, a current `Personnel` mandate may create a durable `Approve`/`Reject` decision that snapshots mandate/manager versions and the effective policy source; no recruitment attempt or candidate mutation occurs before approval. Approval revalidates that exact authority snapshot and then computes a fresh recruitment attempt from current relationships, candidate knowledge, cooldown history, and personnel dependencies rather than storing a pre-rolled outcome. Approved attempts persist the authorizing decision ID and exact mandate/policy provenance; stale approval authority blocks execution while the historical request remains dismissible by rejection. Under `IndependentRecruitment = Delegated`, `Delegated` and `Broad` managers may act autonomously on an authored cadence using only their real Personnel mandate, effective policy, incoming relationship candidates, and their own traits to choose an approach; they do not inspect hidden candidate drives to pick the winning pitch. The resulting attempt uses the same delegated recruitment transaction as an explicit action. If an accepted defection removes a character from another organization, that former organization receives a `Notable` personnel-change report saying only that the member left, without leaking the hidden recruiting organization. Both approval and autonomous paths revalidate authority at commit so stale subordinate actions cannot survive leadership, mandate, or standing-order changes.
- Institutional contacts are persistent person-mediated information channels rather than an influence score. An active contact binds a criminal sponsor organization, one current handler, one external character, that character's current institution, and a typed Police, Legal, Political, Press, Labor, or Professional channel. Establishment requires an actual nonzero directional social relationship and snapshots the relationship and both character versions so later social or membership changes cannot be hidden behind a stale token. Active contacts lock both the handler's sponsor membership and the external contact's institutional membership; explicit termination releases those dependencies while preserving the historical record. Contact disclosure can move only information personally held by the external character into sponsor organization knowledge. The disclosed record keeps the source topic, subject, observation time, reliability, specificity, and summary unchanged, identifies the contact character as its source, retains exact source-information lineage, and uses a channel-specific `PoliceContact`, `Lawyer`, `PoliticalContact`, `Press`, or `ProfessionalContact` source kind. Organization-held institutional truth cannot be read implicitly through a contact, duplicate source disclosures are rejected, and disclosure/contact indexes survive save/load.
- Law-enforcement jurisdiction is persistent institutional state owned by specific law-enforcement or legal-authority organizations. Jurisdictions cover explicit neighborhoods, carry versioned case-intake priority, and have synchronized neighborhood-to-authority indexes. Incident routing chooses the highest-priority active authority deterministically with stable-ID tie breaking; a crime in a neighborhood with no assigned authority does not implicitly create a global police case. Validated operation-resolution tokens snapshot both the routing result, including the absence of an authority, and any chosen jurisdiction version so later jurisdiction changes cannot redirect a stale incident commit.
- Law-enforcement patrol deployment is explicit versioned legal state rather than a neighborhood-wide bonus. Active patrol deployments belong to an active law-enforcement organization inside its current jurisdiction, target one neighborhood, and carry canonical non-overlapping daily windows with bounded local police-presence ratings. Windows may wrap midnight and uncovered minutes resolve to explicit zero deployment presence; the older neighborhood institutional police-presence profile remains the fallback only where no active patrol deployment model exists. Patrols have validated establish/revise/suspend/resume/retire transactions, synchronized organization/neighborhood/active indexes, jurisdiction-contraction guards, stale time/version/jurisdiction checks, and save/load coverage. Operation execution and exposure resolve against the same patrol snapshot, and resolution plans retain every relevant deployment version so a patrol establishment, revision, suspension, or resumption cannot silently change a previously validated outcome.
- Police response is a persistent timed legal event rather than a hidden operation modifier.
  - Authoring: each operation type authors an alert threshold, base/minimum response delay, patrol-delay reduction, optional entry milestone, and explicit difficulty/exposure penalties for response that reaches the target before completion.
  - Dispatch: a qualifying operation dispatches at most one response from the highest-priority active `LawEnforcement` authority with jurisdiction over the target neighborhood; generic legal authorities cannot act as patrol responders. The response snapshots its operation, authority, jurisdiction version, exact authority-specific patrol deployment/version when present, local response-presence rating, alert score, dispatch time, and due arrival time.
  - Delay: active patrol presence deterministically shortens the authored delay while an explicit patrol schedule gap produces zero deployment presence and the slower response; no explicit deployment falls back to the neighborhood institutional profile.
  - Arrivals: due arrivals are indexed by `SimTime`, survive save/load, and are processed before operation resolution so arrival can stale an older resolution token.
  - Pre-entry contingency: `AbortOnPoliceArrivalBeforeEntry` executes as a real standing contingency against an authored entry milestone, including when police arrive while leadership is considering another exception: city time continues, the arrival persists, and a later `Continue` cannot bypass an already-triggered standing abort.
  - After entry: arrival instead applies the authored resolution/exposure penalties, and `RequestDecisionOnUnexpectedCondition` raises a durable `Continue`/`Abort` operation decision whose typed `PoliceArrival(response_id)` reason preserves the exact response provenance.
  - Decision serialization: operation decisions have a historical operation index, response-backed request tokens snapshot the response version, duplicate decisions for one response are rejected, and the typed decision plus its index survive save/load. If police arrive while another operation decision is already pending, no parallel pending decision is fabricated; resolving that earlier decision with `Continue` atomically resumes the operation only long enough to create the police-arrival follow-up at the same simulation time, leaving leadership work serialized and causally complete.
  - Surfacing: immediate response decisions are surfaced by `TickOutcome`, while deferred response decisions are surfaced by `DecisionResolutionOutcome`, so adapters can honor the same attention/auto-pause contract without another notification system.
  - Knowledge: police arrival also creates precise personal police-pressure knowledge for the unique operation participants who actually experience that response, including a standing-order abort; later social systems such as recruitment consume only that character-held knowledge rather than hidden investigation state.
- Surveillance operations now close the information loop instead of resolving as generic objectives with no knowledge consequence. Surveillance authorization requires a `GatherInformation` objective aimed at an observable organization, character, neighborhood, business, operation, investigation, or enterprise. Resolution snapshots only the target state the surveillance integration is allowed to report, rejects stale commits when that target or a relevant patrol deployment changes, and creates organization-held `Surveillance` information linked to the source operation. Achieved observations are specific and generally reliable; partial observations are general and mixed; failed surveillance fabricates no target knowledge. Business surveillance can reveal concrete venue access plus local police activity, neighborhood surveillance can learn approximate recurring patrol windows without exposing deployment IDs, and investigation surveillance reports only visible case activity rather than subjects or evidence. Completed operations persist the exact discovered-information IDs plus a reverse information-to-operation index, and those records can be selected through the normal intelligence path for later operation planning.
- Investigations and evidence are specific persistent records with legal-institution ownership, subjects, custody, causal origin, optional named source, evidence kind, support strength, source reliability, admissibility, explicit derived-evidence lineage, and synchronized case-graph indexes. Evidence can add newly discovered subjects to an existing investigation; reverse indexes support finding every case involving an entity, evidence by kind/origin/source, and every derived record that depends on earlier evidence. Foreign institutions cannot append evidence to another authority's case through the canonical evidence path. Active investigations also support explicit lead and supporting investigator assignments. Staffing requires active same-authority characters with Investigation capability, is indexed both by case and investigator, increments the investigation version, survives save/load, and blocks organization reassignment until the investigator is released. The top-level tick now assigns an unstaffed active case to the owning institution's highest-Investigation-capability available character, with stable-ID tie breaking and one active case per investigator; existing support staff are promoted before an unrelated detective is selected. The assignment still goes through the canonical versioned staffing transaction and does not invent investigative leads or evidence. Assignment tokens snapshot both case and character versions so new evidence, staffing changes, investigative work, or personnel changes cannot be overwritten by stale commits.
- Investigation lifecycle is transactional rather than a passive status flag. Active cases can be suspended or closed; suspended cases can be resumed after their retained staffing is revalidated; closed cases are terminal. Scheduled detective work blocks suspension or closure instead of disappearing. Suspended and closed cases preserve staffing and history but no longer lock investigators to the owning organization. Generic investigation-owner transfer remains intentionally unavailable. Police-to-prosecutor handoff now uses a separate prosecution-referral model that shares exact immutable evidence records without rewriting the source investigation owner or evidence custodian.
- Arrest and custody are now explicit evidence-backed legal records rather than character flags or aliases for conviction. An arrest must identify an active character who is already a subject of an active law-enforcement investigation and cite at least one immutable evidence record from that exact case, held by the arresting authority and directly identifying that character. Arrest validation snapshots the case and character versions, rejects stale commits, enforces one active detention per character through synchronized indexes, and preserves complete historical records after release. Active detention keeps its source investigation open and blocks organization escape, new operation participation, delegated execution, recruitment action, contact handling, investigator work, and new supervisory assignments without erasing formal organization membership, existing reporting lines, mandates, contacts, or case staffing. Existing scheduled detective work and live operations must finish or be explicitly transitioned before an arrest can commit because those execution systems do not yet model interruption. Due enterprise cycles owned by a detained manager remain overdue without executing and resume through the normal deterministic tick path after release. Release is a separate versioned lifecycle transaction, so `Detained` and `Released` remain distinct from future charging, bail, trial, and conviction state.
- Private legal representation is persistent legal state backed by real people, relationships, capabilities, and money rather than a defense bonus. `LegalServices` organizations are distinct from courts, prosecutors, and generic legal authorities; a sponsor can retain only an active `Legal` institutional contact whose external character remains employed by an active legal-services organization and has `LegalKnowledge`. Retention records the exact arrest matter, defendant, sponsor, lawyer, firm, sustaining contact, retainer fee, payer/provider accounts, balanced ledger transaction, optional versioned `Legal` mandate authority, and organization-held legal-activity information/report. One active representation may cover an arrest at a time, active representation prevents silently terminating its sustaining contact, and explicit end transactions preserve an end reason plus separate legal information/report history. Membership is checked when the sponsor retains counsel but is not rewritten into the contract afterward: after release, a represented defendant may later leave the sponsor without corrupting the retained-counsel record. Save/load preserves active/history indexes and fresh representation IDs.
- Prosecutors are now a distinct institution type with their own persistent prosecution-case intake rather than implicit access to police case files. An initial prosecution referral is tied to one arrest, one prosecutor office, one current lead prosecutor with `LegalKnowledge`, the immutable source police investigation/authority, and an explicit evidence set that must include every record used to justify the arrest. The evidence remains police-custodied; the prosecution case gains indexed access only to records actually transferred through durable referral events. Supplemental referrals can add newly developed source-case evidence without duplicating previously shared records, snapshot source-case/lead/case versions, and reject stale commits atomically. Every referral creates prosecutor-held `LegalActivity` information plus a `Legal` report carrying the source authority and case context. One office cannot open duplicate live cases for the same arrest, while a different prosecutor office may receive its own referral. Open cases lock the lead prosecutor's office membership, but detention pauses new prosecutorial work without erasing the formal assignment; work resumes through the same canonical path after release. Release-safe validation reconstructs the exact union and non-overlap of evidence across referrals and verifies every information/report link.
- Case-graph inference resolves deterministic shortest evidence paths using only subject/origin links from evidence already held by that investigation. Stable entity and evidence ordering breaks equal-length ties. The query does not consult hidden social relationships, unrelated world truth, or evidence owned by another case, preserving the knowledge boundary while providing a reusable foundation for investigative reasoning.
- Investigation work is authored and evidence-bounded. `PatternAnalysis` is scheduled against an assigned investigator and a canonical pair of entities, snapshots the case-owned evidence path available when work is authorized, and resolves after an authored six-hour duration; successful work creates a `PatternLink` with exact source-evidence provenance, weak work remains durably inconclusive, and new direct evidence can supersede pending analysis without fabricating another link. `EvidenceReview` operates on one reviewable case-owned evidence record and resolves after an authored three-hour duration; successful review creates a provenance-linked `ForensicAnalysis` that may improve reliability while preserving the source subject, origin, strength, and admissibility and cannot invent a new suspect or relationship. Newly staffed sparse cases can schedule this bounded review automatically instead of stalling with one evidence item. Both work types use the persisted investigation RNG stream, are indexed by due `SimTime`, prevent duplicate/superseded work, reject stale case/personnel dependencies, and preserve deterministic save/load continuation.
- Named witnesses are persistent case records separate from anonymous testimony. A case witness links a concrete character to one investigation with mutable `Hostile`, `Reluctant`, or `Cooperative` state; individual statements persist immutable confidence, summary, subject/origin, time, and a one-to-one testimony evidence record. Named testimony stores the witness as evidence source while preserving causal origin separately, and confidence deterministically maps to evidence strength/reliability. Current witness activity requires an active case and active witness character, while earlier testimony remains valid through suspension and save/load. Anonymous incident testimony remains valid with no named source, so authored operation exposure does not invent a person when the simulation has not identified one.
- Confidential informants are persistent character-to-handler relationships owned by legal state. Establishment and termination use versioned validated tokens, preserve historical relationships, and maintain synchronized character, handler, active-pair, information, case-information, and evidence indexes. Active sources must be terminated before character reassignment can place them inside their handler organization. A disclosure may convert only information personally held by that informant into evidence for a case owned by the handler. The canonical path records exact source-information provenance, deterministically maps information specificity and reliability into evidence strength and reliability, rejects duplicate disclosure of the same information into one case, and prevents generic evidence insertion from fabricating `InformantStatement` records.
- Operation exposure can atomically open a jurisdiction-owned investigation and attach its initial evidence when a resolvable neighborhood has an assigned authority. Operation-originated evidence records the operation as its causal origin, carries exposure-dependent strength/reliability, and may identify a concrete participant at the highest exposure level. The after-action report describes only crew-observable exposure and does not reveal the hidden investigation or evidence graph to the player without a separate intelligence path.
- Reports store player-facing summaries linked to known information, entities, and optional durable decision IDs. Persisted organization reports may cite organization-held information only; character knowledge must first be explicitly transferred into organization knowledge, preventing historical reports from becoming invalid when personnel later leave. Decision-linked reports remain valid after resolution because decision records are historical state.
- Periodic executive briefs are deterministic synthesized reports rather than a second notification queue. An authored registry definition controls the current daily cadence, minimum source attention, and detailed source-entry cap. At a due boundary the top-level tick runs executive synthesis after operational, investigative, business, and enterprise work, so same-minute consequences that have already reached the report layer are eligible. The brief reads only reports addressed to the player organization plus that organization's unresolved decision requests; routine source entries are suppressed, pending decisions are always surfaced ahead of report items, exact duplicate report entries collapse deterministically, and detailed report-derived items are capped with an explicit overflow summary instead of silently disappearing. The previous executive brief's monotonic report ID is the source-window cursor, so old report items are not recycled indefinitely. Plan tokens snapshot the latest report boundary and the exact pending-decision ID/version set, rejecting commits if either changes between synthesis and insertion. Resolved decision-linked source entries are not resurfaced as current work. Cadence is derived from `SimTime` rather than duplicated in mutable schedule state, and save/load immediately before a daily boundary produces the same generated brief.
- Campaign history stores durable entity-linked events for later timelines and campaign recall.
- Finance uses typed monetary accounts and a balanced double-entry-style ledger. Street cash, concealed cash, accounted funds, legitimate operating funds, receivables, payables, and settlement accounts are distinct states rather than one universal cash scalar.
- Property-acquisition operations now have a durable non-cash proceeds lifecycle rather than fabricating an immediate cash payout. Authored operation definitions bound estimated held value and partial-recovery fractions; successful or partial `AcquireProperty` outcomes persist the exact target and estimated held value, while failed or aborted operations create no property. Held property remains organizational value but not spendable cash until an explicit disposition transaction. Disposition requires an active organization-owned business with `ResaleMarket` capability, distinct open organization-owned liquid-cash and settlement accounts, and an authored liquidation recovery rate. Commit atomically records a balanced ledger transaction, accountant information, a notable financial report, the resale venue plus its historical ownership version, realized value, and operation disposition provenance; a property record can be disposed only once. Organization financial reporting separates currently held property from period liquidation cash and executive synthesis refreshes an earlier acquisition after-action when the asset has since been liquidated without duplicating same-window disposition noise.
- Multi-account financial transactions validate every posting and resulting balance before a consuming commit token atomically changes any account. Account versions prevent stale validated transactions from overwriting newer balances.
- Mandate budgets are tied to ledger history rather than duplicated spend counters. A budget specifies an organization-owned funding account, a limit, and a daily or weekly period. Delegated transactions explicitly identify the mandate manager and responsibility scope exercising authority, persist that authority snapshot with the mandate version, enforce scope and manager ownership before mutation, and reject stale mandate or manager snapshots.
- Routine criminal enterprises are persistent delegated records rather than repeated player commands. The current authored enterprise set contains protection, gambling, and alcohol distribution. Establishment binds an organization, manager mandate, responsibility scope, location, an explicit set of supporting businesses where the racket needs a wider commercial network, cash account, and permanently exclusive settlement account; lifecycle supports active, suspended, and closed states with versioned consuming transitions.
- Enterprise economics are immutable registry definitions. Daily cycles derive gross revenue and operating cost from authored economics plus neighborhood illicit demand, commercial activity, wealth, police presence, manager Management capability, bounded state-owned RNG variance, and any relevant standing policy snapshot. Results persist gross, cost, net, variance, manager capability, policy, attention class, ledger transaction, and optional information provenance rather than collapsing to a universal profit scalar.
- Active enterprise work is indexed by next-cycle `SimTime` in deterministic order, so the minute pipeline processes due work without scanning every enterprise. Establishment and resume tokens carry authored cycle duration rather than a precomputed timestamp, anchoring the next cycle to actual commit time even when validation and commit are separated. Support-business versions are also snapshotted, so a validated establishment or resume cannot survive a later ownership change. A due enterprise whose current manager is detained stays overdue but is excluded from executable due work until that manager is released, preserving the formal mandate without letting custody produce invalid autonomous actions. Cycle settlement uses the normal balanced ledger transaction path and then advances enterprise history/schedule atomically.
- Enterprise performance participates in the attention model without inventing crises: normal variance is `Routine`; authored material-variance thresholds produce `Notable` cycles. A notable cycle creates precise `AfterAction` information held by the owning organization and linked to its manager and enterprise. Routine cycles remain compact financial history.
- Enterprise financial reporting is derived on demand from cycle history rather than cached totals. It supports drill-down by enterprise/racket, organization, manager, exact location, and neighborhood, including gross revenue, operating cost, net cash, cycle count, and notable-cycle count. Historical windows exclude enterprises not yet established by the window end. Generated enterprise financial reports contain a routine aggregate plus chronological notable entries whose sources are the organization's after-action information records.
- Legitimate businesses can run independent operating economies through a separate `EconomyState`. Each operating business owns distinct legitimate-operating and settlement accounts, a deterministic due-time schedule, versioned lifecycle, and durable cycle history. Business title is independently versioned and historical: creation records the initial owner, validated transfers atomically update current organization/character ownership indexes plus reverse historical-owner indexes and append immutable ownership-change records, and stale transfer tokens cannot overwrite newer title. Ownership intervals use transfer instants as half-open boundaries, so a former owner does not retain a zero-duration historical overlap at the exact transfer minute. Establishment and resume schedules begin from actual commit time, and historical summaries exclude operating economies not yet established by the requested window end. Authored business economics consume neighborhood wealth and commercial activity; material variances produce owner-scoped accountant information rather than hidden truth. Every business cycle snapshots the business ownership version and owner at commit, so later transfers cannot rewrite historical income attribution or redirect already-planned accounting information. Organization financial summaries and notable financial reports resolve legitimate business history from those persisted ownership snapshots rather than the business's current title. Organization financial reporting compares legitimate business cashflow with illicit enterprise cashflow from persisted cycle history.
- Business-backed enterprises consume concrete business functions rather than generic percentage bonuses. Host requirements remain local to a specific venue, so authored gambling still requires its hosting business to provide cash handling, meeting space, and customer access. Network requirements are resolved from the union of a hosted business plus explicitly assigned support businesses. Alcohol distribution therefore requires organization-owned vehicle fleet, warehousing, distribution infrastructure, and customer access capacity that may be spread across multiple businesses. Active rackets lock supporting-business ownership against transfer outside the organization; suspension releases that lock, while resumption requires the network to be valid again. Reverse indexes allow every racket supported by a business to be found without scanning enterprise state, and all host/network requirements are checked at establishment, cycle planning, resume, and save/load against the immutable registry.
- Save envelopes are versioned, content-revision checked, structurally and registry validated, and preserve every persisted RNG stream and cross-system continuation boundary. Persistence compatibility is current-version only: restore rejects a mismatched save-format version, state-schema version, or authored content revision, and there is no implicit migration/defaulting path for older continuation state. Release-safe validation also verifies that every typed ID allocator remains strictly ahead of its persisted record high-water mark, preventing malformed saves from reusing an existing ID after restore. The current authored content revision is 13.
- The current in-memory state schema version is 39. ## Architectural Boundaries

- Core systems contain no file, network, database, UI, or process IO.
- Registries contain definitions only; generated campaign values remain in `AppState` or owned records.
- Indexes are derived state and are private to the owner that keeps them synchronized.
- Consequential cross-record mutation uses validation before commit. Multi-resource transactions use consuming validated tokens. Tokens that depend on mutable records capture record versions and reject stale commits; dependencies that can change independently of the primary record are revalidated at commit.
- Player knowledge is represented separately from world truth. Reports reference information records rather than reading hidden state as if it were known.
- There is no universal heat value. Legal pressure is represented by actual investigations and evidence.
- There is no tactical movement or combat-control layer in the core architecture.
- Core predicate naming follows `is_`, `has_`, and `can_`; synchronized-index validation uses `has_consistent_indexes` project-wide.

## Intentionally Not Yet Modeled

The foundation deliberately does not fabricate shallow implementations for systems whose data model needs further design. Major domains still to be introduced through their own records, registries, systems, indexes, invariants, persistence coverage, and behavioral tests include:

- City geography beyond neighborhood economic/institutional profiles: transport topology, population, richer institution networks, and schedules beyond the explicit daily patrol windows now modeled by law-enforcement deployments.
- Employment, wages, detailed supply chains, acquisition pricing and purchase consideration, laundering plausibility, persistent asset quantities/condition beyond operation-held property value, market competition, and broader citywide supply/demand. Legitimate business operating cycles, categorical asset functions, versioned ownership transfer/history, and an explicit operation-property resale transaction now exist, but title transfer intentionally does not fabricate a purchase price, financing, or payment transaction and property resale does not yet model buyer demand, resale capacity, seizure, delayed disposition, or adverse venue-specific pricing. Protection, gambling, and alcohol distribution now prove the routine illicit-enterprise foundation. Alcohol distribution requires an explicit organization-owned business network providing transport, storage, distribution, and retail-access capabilities, but it intentionally does not yet invent supplier contracts, inventory quantities, shipment throughput, wholesale purchasing, spoilage, route topology, or dynamic market pricing. Bookmaking, loan sharking, fencing as a recurring enterprise, labor racketeering, fraud, and other authored enterprise types remain unmodeled.
- Delegated role staffing selection, responsibility handoff, personnel requirements, dependency resolution, and richer manager behavior beyond the current mandate-driven routine enterprise cycles and bounded autonomous Personnel recruitment. Recruitment candidates remain relationship-gated and autonomous approaches are selected from the manager's own authored traits rather than inferred from hidden candidate motives; broader goal selection, resource competition, diplomacy, territory strategy, and multi-step rival planning are still not modeled.
- Broader character need satisfaction, wages/income expectations, family ties, secrets, injuries, personal legal progression beyond the current evidence-backed detention/release lifecycle, non-criminal employment/specialist affiliation, contact-type recruitment networks, and general behavior decision systems. Drives now provide authored causal inputs to recruitment, but they are not a universal satisfaction meter and do not yet generate unrelated betrayal, theft, or family-pressure events.
- Operation equipment and reusable vehicles, injuries/casualties, damaged or recovered assets, explicit bad-intelligence misinformation effects, tactical police routing/travel topology, dispatch-capacity competition, pursuit, and richer condition-specific exposure remain unmodeled. Timed jurisdictional response now exists as an authored persistent event tied to local patrol presence and operation contingencies, but it intentionally does not fabricate street-level movement, unit inventories, pursuit physics, or tactical combat. Started aborts preserve their causal phase, authority, response, or decision provenance and after-action information/report/history, but they still do not infer partial objective progress, exposure/evidence, injuries, casualties, or asset consequences without dedicated simulation inputs.
- Broader autonomous detective lead generation beyond evidence review and case-graph pattern work, selection/prioritization among multiple possible investigative actions, case merging, institutional investigative budgets beyond one-active-case investigator capacity, witness intimidation/protection and availability over time, autonomous informant recruitment/coercion/payment/exposure behavior, broader inter-authority case/evidence sharing beyond the explicit police-to-prosecutor referral path, charging, bail, trial, conviction/acquittal, court procedure, corruption/favors, political procedures, press behavior, labor institutions, and broader rival strategy remain unmodeled. Prosecutor offices, lead assignments, exact referred-evidence access, retained private counsel, and legal spending now exist without treating arrest as guilt or collapsing public and private legal institutions together. Charging is intentionally not inferred merely from evidence strength: current evidence records identify subject, causal origin/source, kind, strength, reliability, and admissibility, but do not yet carry an authored legal allegation/proposition stating what offense that evidence proves. A future charging model needs that semantic layer plus its own authored prosecution rules instead of reusing detective-work scoring or inventing a generic criminal code in transaction logic. Institutions still do not invent a lead, identity, relationship, charge, or external forensic database when the evidence graph does not support one. Institutional contacts provide typed provenance-preserving information channels into legal, police, prosecutor, political, press, labor, civic, and commercial organizations, but they intentionally do not imply favor access, corruption success, institutional authority, or omniscient case knowledge.
- Broader organizational approvals beyond the modeled recruitment proposal remain intentionally typed by domain rather than collapsed into a universal approval flag. Recruitment now proves the shared durable-decision foundation with its own `Approve`/`Reject` contract; future budget, staffing, diplomacy, or other proposal domains still need explicit context, authority, result, and stale-dependency semantics before joining it. `WithinBudget` recruitment is not inferred because recruitment currently has no authored monetary cost model to evaluate against a mandate budget.
- Autonomous opportunity generation from broader economic, social, territorial, and institutional changes, campaign pressures, historical transitions, and end-state evaluation. The persistent opportunity/lifecycle/report/conversion foundation now exists, but generators remain intentionally domain-specific and must supply causal organization-held source information rather than spawning random missions.

These omissions are intentional. New systems should be added only when their canonical ownership and transaction boundaries are clear enough to preserve the design law.
