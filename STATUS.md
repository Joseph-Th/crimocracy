## Runtime foundation

- `AppState` owns serializable campaign state, simulation time, typed ID counters, attention settings, and independent deterministic RNG streams for operation, investigation, business, and enterprise work. Autonomous recruitment is deterministic managerial judgment and consumes no RNG.
- `Registry` owns immutable Rust-authored definitions. Runtime records and generated values belong to `AppState` and its domain owners.
- Save/load validates envelope, schema, authored content revision, registry references, cross-references, indexes, and ID high-water marks before accepting state. Compatibility is current-version only; no implicit migration or defaulting.
- [`core::simulation::run_tick`](src/core/simulation.rs) is the canonical one-minute pipeline: it processes due work in stable order and returns `Result<TickOutcome, TickError>`, rejecting an exhausted simulation clock before any phase mutates state.
- `validate_state` provides release-safe structural checks; debug boundaries additionally run the full invariant validator.
- Historical business ownership is queried through the reconstructed business/version index with time-bounded searches rather than a full ownership replay for each operation, opportunity, or financial-report query. Same-minute ownership ambiguity and the established reporting-interval semantics are preserved, including after save restoration; the save schema is unchanged.
- Historical jurisdiction and patrol snapshots use ordered revision boundaries and version-ordinal lookups, avoiding repeated full-history scans in police-response validation and patrol presence queries. Same-minute routing ambiguity and the final effective revision within a minute are preserved across saves.
- Due legitimate-business settlements isolate businesses whose own finite version or artifact capacity is exhausted, without consuming their random draws or preventing independent businesses from earning. The remaining viable same-minute cohort still preflights its complete shared ID budget before any settlement, so global scarcity never selects an arbitrary business-ID prefix.
- Due detective work uses the same isolation rule: a permanently unallocatable evidence or interview outcome remains scheduled without consuming its investigation RNG draw, while other independently resolvable cases finish. The complete viable work cohort still reserves shared artifact IDs before any case mutates.
- Daily criminal-organization payroll isolates an organization whose own required wage-account, ledger, or shortfall-report IDs are exhausted, without withholding wages from unrelated funded organizations. Feasible payrolls still reserve their entire combined ID budget before the first payment, so a genuinely shared shortfall cannot pay only an ID-ordered prefix.

## Player and evaluation adapters

- A first-class `city_generation::generate_city` builds seed-stable playable cities through the production world, economy, finance, jurisdiction, patrol, delegation, contacts, social, enterprise, intelligence and opportunity owners. Small/medium/large presets create 4/8/16 districts, 48/144/384 modeled businesses, 2/3/5 competing criminal outfits, and 1/2/4 staffed police precincts; validated custom configs allow 4-24 districts, 8-40 businesses per district and 1-8 rival outfits. Neighborhood names/profiles, industry clusters, business owners, civilians, institutional specialists, crew identities and relations vary by seed. Every modeled storefront has independently attached operating/settlement books; each district has explicit police case intake and two patrol windows. Criminal rivals have autonomous expansion mandates, genuine treasury funding and active local rackets. The player has a real crew, cash front, resale venue, connected recruitment prospects, live police and legal acquaintances, and two burglary leads. The visual city scene projects roads, lots and building locations deterministically from these authoritative records. Generated mode is opt-in with `cargo text-play --generate --seed N --city-size small|medium|large`; authored scenarios remain supported, and current-schema saves restore either world directly. The generated city is a complete simulated economic/institutional starting environment, not yet a topology-aware transport, citizen mobility or traffic system; the WebGL2 scene is read-only presentation. Debug long advances incur whole-state invariant checks per minute.
- The text player can debrief one current, non-detained member's personally held observation at a time through the production intelligence transfer validator. `debriefs` counts notes not already transferred; `debrief` records an internal report retaining the original source, observation time and typed signal. `intel` shows full details only for organization-held information, never a guessed outsider or staff-only source. Police-contact disclosures can convey a specific investigator-held case-witness signal after the simulator creates an actual incident witness; once learned, that status can authorize witness pressure subject to the production legal checks. Automatic operation staffing uses globally optimal, trained, one-member-per-role assignment with deterministic ties instead of a greedy role-by-role choice; manual assignment still permits knowingly untrained members.
- Player operation planning accepts the production surveillance/infiltration target shapes: public districts/businesses and identified people, institutions, operations, cases and enterprises. A knowledge-bounded `known-targets` view derives those IDs from player-owned records, contacts, real recruitment prospects, and organization-held intelligence, not hidden world indices. Completed precinct surveillance can reveal case-activity information generated by the legal system after a player operation, without disclosing unknown case identifiers. The `plan` command also exposes relative start delays, completion deadlines, registry-relevant intelligence prerequisites, and pre-entry abort/leadership-decision police contingencies; owner validation supplies all crew-booking, timing, evidence-freshness and target constraints. `cancel` calls the production operation abort transition on authorized/in-progress owned operations, recording debrief provenance and releasing personnel. Arbitrarily guessed administrative IDs never permit recon plans. Dedicated proactive witness/detention investigation acquisition remains outside this adapter.
- Player economic orders include business suspension and resumption through legitimate-economy lifecycle validation, with active racket hosting/support dependencies enforced, and enterprise establishment at either an eligible physical host or a district using multiple player-owned support businesses. District placement is restricted in the client to rackets whose registry definition has no physical-host requirement; accounts, mandates and all business-function prerequisites are validated by the enterprise owner. The terminal also exposes the player organization's account-scoped ledger, bounded newest-first transaction history, owned-account-only transaction details, and actual business/enterprise settlement histories. Business cycles settled under another owner are excluded, and historic records, delegates and laundering budgets survive save/restore. Read-only finance projections follow the indexed account chronology instead of walking rival transactions.
- Text play now includes player-managed institutional ties and legal defense. `connections` shows only identified outsiders with real staff relationships; `connect` establishes an owned institutional channel, while `contacts` and `contact` inspect, disclose from, and terminate channels without exposing undisclosed intelligence. `counsel` lists known current-member arrests and representation, `retain` pays a legal-services provider using actual liquid funds, and `end-counsel` enforces case and representation lifecycle rules. The authored opening now includes independent counsel and two genuine staff acquaintance links, but not a free lawyer or automatic support. Player time advancement pauses on actual member arrest and release events before skipping the manual legal-response window. `policy legal automatic` remains the canonical per-tick automatic retention path.
- `cargo text-play` starts a seeded, line-oriented leadership session using either a procedural city or one of six authored city openings. The interactive and scripted client presents public districts and business-directory identities, the player's reports, held intelligence, known personnel and recruitment prospects, owned businesses and enterprises, finances, opportunities, and decisions. Players select current members for scouting/burglary, discover new leads from reconnaissance, consult contacts, recruit, buy businesses with accounted funds, launder through owned fronts, sweep earned profits, open/allocate same-kind accounts, establish and manage delegated rackets, dispose of property or deposit cash, resolve decisions, and advance canonical simulated time. Governance commands set organization-level recruitment/legal-support policies, assign/revise/revoke managerial mandates, manage standing orders, and limit daily/weekly delegated budgets.
- The client plans registry-defined operations with skill-based or explicit crew assignment and supported approaches. Automatic staffing rejects untrained required roles; explicit player assignments may take that risk. Person-targeted extraction and witness pressure require identified people and canonical episode-bound learned legal status, not knowledge inferred from hidden cases or guessed IDs. The text client remains the primary player-experience inspection surface, with strict command rejection and current-version campaign saves under `target/text-play/`; load restores the saved state directly. Real play through this client, not a scripted policy, is how player experience is judged.

## Cross-cutting guarantees

- One owning system and one canonical path per consequential mutation.
- Fallible multi-record operations validate references, ownership, lifecycle, permissions, capacity, arithmetic, and versions before commit.
- Rejected operations preserve authoritative state except for explicitly modeled diagnostics or audit records.
- Derived indexes and summaries are maintained only from authoritative records and checked for consistency. They are omitted from save bytes and rebuilt deterministically from records during restore before invariant validation. An ID obtained from an owner-maintained index must resolve to its authoritative record; missing indexed targets are invalid state, not ordinary absence to filter away.
- Result-affecting randomness uses serialized state-owned streams; ordered work uses explicit stable ordering.
- Player-visible information is separate from hidden world truth. Reports point to information records; adapters and text play do not grant omniscience.
- Filesystem, process, network, and UI effects remain outside core systems.
- The text play client exercises production paths with procedural cities and authored openings. It does not prove human comprehension or interface quality.

## Explicit exclusions

The foundation does not model:

- detailed city geography, transport topology, population simulation, broad institution networks, supply chains, inventory quantities, negotiated or dynamic acquisition pricing, market competition, or dynamic citywide supply and demand;
- broader employment, wages, family ties, secrets, injuries, general character needs, specialist affiliations, or universal autonomous behavior;
- delegated role staffing, broad resource competition beyond overlapping character assignments, diplomacy, territory strategy, multi-step rival planning, or general campaign-pressure generation;
- durable receivable/payable obligations for unpaid AI-organization payroll: a shortfall models its consequence through crew resentment (and, for the player organization, a shortfall report), not as carried debt;
- reusable equipment and vehicles, tactical movement, combat control, pursuit, dispatch capacity, casualties, injuries, asset damage, or condition-specific tactical consequences;
- autonomous investigative lead generation beyond modeled evidence review and case-graph work, case merging, charging, bail, trial, conviction, acquittal, court procedure, corruption, political procedure, press behavior, or labor institutions;
- a universal approval flag, generic legal-pressure meter, or generic mission-card generator. Modeled approvals, legal pressure, and opportunities remain typed and domain-owned.

These are scope boundaries, not evidence for unmodeled design goals.

Legal-support policy has only the two executable states: case-by-case/manual handling and automatic retention. Unsupported tactical, governance, or policy axes are not represented as inert plan fields.

Expired or converted operation opportunities require a newer observation of an actionable target before discovery can advertise another opening of the same kind. Dismissal remains a reversible leadership choice and does not consume the opening. Save validation checks the same historical freshness rule.
