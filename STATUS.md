## Runtime foundation

- `AppState` owns serializable campaign state, simulation time, typed ID counters, attention settings, and independent deterministic RNG streams for operation, investigation, business, and enterprise work. Autonomous recruitment is deterministic managerial judgment and consumes no RNG.
- `Registry` owns immutable Rust-authored definitions. Runtime records and generated values belong to `AppState` and its domain owners.
- Save/load validates envelope, schema, authored content revision, registry references, cross-references, indexes, and ID high-water marks before accepting state. Compatibility is current-version only; no implicit migration or defaulting.
- [`core::simulation::run_tick`](src/core/simulation.rs) is the canonical one-minute pipeline: it processes due work in stable order and returns `Result<TickOutcome, TickError>`, rejecting an exhausted simulation clock before any phase mutates state.
- `validate_state` provides release-safe structural checks; debug boundaries additionally run the full invariant validator.
- Historical business ownership is queried through the reconstructed business/version index with time-bounded searches rather than a full ownership replay for each operation, opportunity, or financial-report query. Same-minute ownership ambiguity and the established reporting-interval semantics are preserved, including after save restoration; the save schema is unchanged.
- Historical jurisdiction and patrol snapshots use ordered revision boundaries and version-ordinal lookups, avoiding repeated full-history scans in police-response validation and patrol presence queries. Same-minute routing ambiguity and the final effective revision within a minute are preserved across saves.
- Due legitimate-business settlements isolate businesses whose own finite version or artifact capacity is exhausted, without consuming their random draws or preventing independent businesses from earning. The remaining viable same-minute cohort still preflights its complete shared ID budget before any settlement, so global scarcity never selects an arbitrary business-ID prefix.
- Due detective work uses the same isolation rule: a permanently unallocatable evidence or interview outcome remains scheduled without consuming its investigation RNG draw, while other independently resolvable cases finish. The complete viable work cohort still reserves shared artifact IDs before any case mutates.
- Daily criminal-organization payroll isolates an organization whose own required wage-account, ledger, or shortfall-report IDs are exhausted, without withholding wages from unrelated funded organizations. Feasible payrolls still reserve their entire combined ID budget before the first payment, so a genuinely shared shortfall cannot pay only an ID-ordered prefix.
- Non-player legitimate-business recovery uses a zero-variance counterfactual for the owner's entire active local portfolio, not just the reopened storefront. A shop must add net daily owner earnings after accounting for lost sales at the owner's existing related shops; competitors under other ownership do not count against the owner's decision. Only the strongest competing-sector recovery is selected each day, and an exhausted economy version that cannot schedule another settlement stays suspended. Player-controlled recovery remains manual and uses the canonical lifecycle validator.

## Player and evaluation adapters

- Three optional, explicit sandbox victory claims are available through `victory` and `claim-victory`: political capture needs a 30-day political contact, at least two distinct kinds of 90-day continuously owned operating businesses with 20 profitable cycles **within the last 30 days** per qualifying district, at least one member's wages in mature net income per district, a distinct undetained, delegated political district organizer on a combined Political-and-Neighborhood mandate in each of three quarters of districts, a named cadre of at least two headquarters members plus one delegate per required district, and overall mature legitimate income covering this larger organization's full wage bill; retirement needs net accounted reserves funding a $1,000,000 executive nest egg plus 180 days of registry-authored wages for each subordinate through a balanced personal-account ledger transaction; city underboss needs 90-day rackets with 20 profitable cycles **within 30 days** in three quarters of districts, each district earning a member's wages, the mature rackets outnumbering the combined rival local presence, and the mature racket portfolio covering payroll, with more active rackets than any individual rival citywide. Underboss also requires at least ten named members or four headquarters members plus two per district required for three-quarters territorial control, whichever is larger, and two independent managers of mature active rackets in each qualifying district, each with 20 personally directed profitable settlements within 30 days; a manager can fill only one district captain post, and recent reassignments cannot inherit their predecessor's command record. The greater staff payroll must still be covered by earned racket income. Qualifying businesses and rackets also require a settlement within seven days. Income uses actual settled operating net, including losses, not estimates, laundering, property sales, or balances. This makes civic and territorial leadership earned operating outcomes rather than same-day contact, purchase, or racket-placement achievements. Merely qualifying does not stop the simulation. A successful claim appends a typed history event and recipient report, and permanently terminates further ticks; save/restore reconstructs the ending from history without changing the serialized state layout. The political ending abstracts a municipal coalition and does not simulate an actual electoral office or elections. Underboss standings use world truth only in the validator; the player-facing progress view does not disclose unrevealed rival positions.

- Criminal rackets now own persistently tracked aggregate rank-and-file associates distinct from named management. Each kind has a baseline crew included in existing fixed operating costs, and player orders can hire up to eight further associates per racket. Each additional associate costs 1,500 cents per daily settlement and adds gross according to actual local illicit demand, commercial activity and wealth. These wage/output effects are included in production cycles, previews, NPC financial projections, historical settlement re-derivation and save/load; staff changes retain a versioned as-of-settlement history and reject stale or due-cycle orders without mutation. Managerial span now derives from actual active enterprise work and the assigned captain's Management skill, rather than a fixed count of rackets, associates, or total organization headcount. Each racket, each staffed associate, each supporting storefront, and every additional district in one captain's portfolio contributes to a recurring supervision workload. The skill rating sets their ongoing capacity, with a baseline allowance based solely on a racket kind's required initial staff, never its optional additional hires. Supervisory responsibilities propagate upward through the actual membership tree: each superior directs the distinct immediate operating branches beneath them, with Tight/Guided subordinates consuming more effort than Delegated/Broad ones. Thus a weak boss cannot personally command unlimited independent captains, whereas a qualified underboss can group several captains under one reporting branch and distribute actual work across the organization. Hiring idle people does not add command capacity, and same-organization supervisor changes now permit live mandates and assignments to remain intact while held manager-version tokens correctly stale. Establishment, staff changes, reassignments, recovery and NPC expansion validate the entire current reporting chain; the autonomous same-day expansion cohort additionally preflights the combined projected workload of all candidates against shared supervisors before any establishment commits. Day-boundary maintenance can suspend overextended active portfolios, preferring the smallest excess subordinate command branch before unrelated established rackets, without invalidating otherwise structurally valid older saves. Arrest custody already shuts dependent rackets through the canonical legal consequences; management capacity does not duplicate that transition. Suspended racket employees cease drawing additional operational wages until resumption. Underboss control also requires 12 proven associates per qualifying district, earned by 20 profitable cycles at their current headcount under the current captain, and more active rank-and-file associates locally than all rivals combined; opening a fresh racket or hiring immediately before claiming victory is insufficient. The text client exposes active/suspended associates, all-tier command workload, individual captain and supervisor reporting duties through `people`, per-racket headcount and `staff-racket` governance through the canonical enterprise owner. Profits from unrelated legitimate stores continue to distribute without an artificial per-person dollar withdrawal limit because those businesses have independent employees.

It does not disclose current hidden cases or guarantee profitability. Known business leads omit owned venues and unrelated accounting observations.

- Criminal territory is actual active-racket presence, with strict leaders and contested ties. `turf` shows the player's current racket and associate deployment, discovered rival operations and 30-day standstills. Agreement history persists and prevents new rackets and offensive business orders on both sides, while preserving existing suspended rackets' right to resume after conflict penalties. Commercial acquisition and reconnaissance remain possible. The system models gang pressure and racket displacement, not individual gun battles, permanent land transfers or protected district ownership flags.

## Adversarial pressure

- Legitimate storefronts have a shared local market within each district and business kind: 60% of potential is venue-specific and 40% is contested, divided by effective active competitor capacity. Damage reduces a venue's capacity; healthy competitors recover a share of its lost custom, up to their normal gross. Ownership does not affect the formula. Actual settlement and live laundering use this market context; acquisition forecasts assume all directory peers operate normally and do not reveal private books. NPC recovery ranks viable reopenings by projected daily net cash, using business ID only for ties, and admits at most one reopening among competing local trades per daily boundary. Cycle history freezes the market snapshot, and held single-cycle plans stale when market capacity changes. Physical operation take potential remains a distinct venue-scale abstraction.

- Generated criminal rivals have Operations mandates and trained scouts. Once per day, an eligible NPC outfit can authorize at most one commercial-pressure operation against a foreign storefront in a sector and district where it operates an active storefront. Target selection uses the public directory and its own holdings, not hidden opponent finances or ownership. It scouts first and can sabotage only with usable organization-held reconnaissance; failed attempts have a retry cooldown, and capable, available crew and acceptable police fear are required. These operations use normal execution, police response, evidence, and business-disruption settlement. The player receives observable business consequences, not automatic attribution to a rival. Civilian competitors are eligible targets too.
- On daily boundaries, available prosecutors take one intake, supplement, screening, or court-presentation action each. Arrests with no prior prosecution episode queue by current detention and age; the same police file and defendant do not gain duplicate parallel autonomous reviews. Own-member notices persist detention history and allow counsel to continue after release. Fully supplemented inactive files with insufficient admissible corroboration are declined after release. Screened files can reach an abstract independent court after fourteen days of preparation and release from all custody, without waiting indefinitely for ongoing investigation supplements. Court proof requires at least two independent credible admissible conduct sources of Strong or Direct strength, and a weighted proof score of eight or more, increased by up to two for retained counsel's LegalKnowledge. Known association, weaker reliability, tainted exhibits and duplicate derivatives do not count as independent conduct proof. Later judgments on a police file exclude primary evidence from periods already adjudicated for that defendant. A conviction imposes a personal fine of sixty authored daily wages; acquittal imposes none. Outstanding debt is derived from the judgment and linked balanced ledger payments. Daily collection after payroll debits only the defendant's accessible street/accounted cash, credits an institutional settlement counterparty, and leaves concealed cash and employer assets untouched. Judgments, counsel and payment history persist; restore re-derives proof and fine and validates ledger attribution. The text client shows own-member court outcomes and outstanding fines through `counsel`. This is an abstract hearing and monetary sentence, not imprisonment, charge-specific procedure, asset-network forfeiture or extended investigative detention.
- Staffed active vice inquiries can collect personal surveillance evidence on subsequent settled racket cycles. First exposure remains weak enterprise-only evidence. An available lead detective can attempt at most one observation per minute, with success chance equal to Investigation skill multiplied by `(100 + police presence) / 200`. A successful distinct observed operating event identifies the enterprise's assigned manager with Strong, Credible, Admissible primary evidence; the same cycle cannot create duplicate corroboration, and forensic derivatives remain the same source. Suspended or retired rackets do not generate new operating events. The next minute's ordinary custody gate evaluates new evidence. To prevent repeated routine detention from unrelated vice files every time another daily racket cycle is observed, autonomous custody uses the most recent actual release of that person by the same police authority: routine new detention waits fourteen days, while genuinely new resolved criminal-operation evidence remains immediately actionable. Direct validated arrests continue to use the existing evidence/custody rules. The policy is recomputed from persistent arrest records across save/load and creates no hidden heat or age-based economic pressure. Historical validation checks the prior inquiry, unique cycle/time and frozen manager identity.
- Evidence-backed arrest atomically suspends every active enterprise managed by the detainee and reports the resulting shutdown to its owner. Other managers' enterprises are unaffected. Leadership can transfer active or suspended rackets to another eligible mandate without changing their accounts, identity, cycle history or current schedule; earlier settlements retain their historical manager. A suspended racket earns no overdue payout at release; player recovery requires explicit resumption, and NPC recovery follows the existing daily authority, economic and working-capital checks. Arrest is not conviction, and ordinary legitimate storefront ownership is not confiscated.

## Cross-cutting guarantees

- One owning system and one canonical path per consequential mutation.
- Fallible multi-record operations validate references, ownership, lifecycle, permissions, capacity, arithmetic, and versions before commit.
- Rejected operations preserve authoritative state except for explicitly modeled diagnostics or audit records.
- Derived indexes and summaries are maintained only from authoritative records and checked for consistency. They are omitted from save bytes and rebuilt deterministically from records during restore before invariant validation. An ID obtained from an owner-maintained index must resolve to its authoritative record; missing indexed targets are invalid state, not ordinary absence to filter away.
- Result-affecting randomness uses serialized state-owned streams; ordered work uses explicit stable ordering.
- Player-visible information is separate from hidden world truth. Reports point to information records; adapters and text play do not grant omniscience.
- Filesystem, process, network, and UI effects remain outside core systems.
- The text play client exercises production paths with procedural cities and authored openings. It does not prove human comprehension or interface quality.

## Explicit exclusions

The foundation does not model:

- detailed city geography, transport topology, population simulation, broad institution networks, supply chains, inventory quantities, negotiated or dynamic acquisition pricing, detailed product-level market competition, or dynamic citywide supply and demand;
- broader employment, wages, family ties, secrets, injuries, general character needs, specialist affiliations, or universal autonomous behavior;
- general delegated role staffing, broad resource competition beyond overlapping character assignments, diplomacy, territory strategy, multi-step rival planning beyond the bounded commercial reconnaissance/strike loop, or general campaign-pressure generation;
- durable receivable/payable obligations for unpaid AI-organization payroll: a shortfall models its consequence through crew resentment (and, for the player organization, a shortfall report), not as carried debt;
- reusable equipment and vehicles, tactical movement, combat control, pursuit, dispatch capacity, casualties, injuries, asset damage, or condition-specific tactical consequences;
- autonomous investigative lead generation beyond modeled evidence review, vice observation and case-graph work, case merging, charge-specific adjudication, bail, detailed trial/court procedure, incarceration, organizational asset forfeiture, corruption, political procedure, press behavior, or labor institutions;
- a universal approval flag, generic legal-pressure meter, or generic mission-card generator. Modeled approvals, legal pressure, and opportunities remain typed and domain-owned.

These are scope boundaries, not evidence for unmodeled design goals.
