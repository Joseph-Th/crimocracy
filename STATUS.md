# Crimocracy Foundation Status

This file records the architectural foundation currently present in the repository. It is not a feature roadmap and does not override `AGENTS.md` or `GAME_DESIGN.md`.

## Current Foundation

- Deterministic, serializable `AppState` owns all mutable campaign state and the state-owned RNG.
- Persistent references use typed `u32` IDs allocated by state-owned counters.
- Immutable authored definitions are assembled by the startup `Registry` from Rust content builders.
- World records cover organizations, characters, neighborhoods, and businesses with synchronized lookup indexes. Neighborhoods carry explicit economic and institutional profiles for wealth, commercial activity, illicit demand, police presence, political influence, social cohesion, and tolerance for visible violence. Businesses carry authored kinds plus concrete organizational functions such as cash intensity, vehicle access, warehousing, meeting space, customer access, union access, distribution infrastructure, and professional records rather than percentage-bonus upgrades.
- Character organization membership and supervision changes use versioned validated reassignment tokens. Commit revalidates the current hierarchy and active dependencies, so later supervisor, mandate, operation, investigation, or direct-report changes cannot make an older token bypass organization-integrity rules. Organization changes require direct reports to be reassigned first and are rejected while the character owns active operational, delegated, or investigative responsibility.
- Directional relationships are persistent records with bounded dimensions and a single mutation system.
- Intelligence is stored as provenance-bearing records with holder, source, typed topic, subject, observation time, reliability, specificity, and explicit source-information lineage. Typed topics include target security, personnel, schedules, police activity, routes, relationships, financial performance, legal activity, market access, and operational outcomes. Holder/topic indexes support focused retrieval without scanning all knowledge. Original information cannot author lineage or masquerade as an internal report; only the canonical versioned transfer path can create an `InternalReport`, with exactly one source-information record, a source entity matching that source holder, and the same topic as its source. Transfers are tied to current organizational relationships, transferred records remain organization-held historical knowledge after the reporting character later leaves, and reverse lineage indexes support traceability from source information to derived internal reports.
- Semantic operations model objective, approach, leader, roles, explicitly selected planning intelligence, constraints, contingencies, schedule, execution timing, and lifecycle. Authorization uses a consuming validated token that snapshots leader and role-participant versions, requires active participants, rejects commits after dependent personnel change or the scheduled authorization time has passed, and accepts intelligence only when it is already held by the responsible organization and relevant to the authored operation plan.
- Operation execution profiles are immutable registry definitions grouped into difficulty, intelligence, and exposure profiles. Authored difficulty definitions provide duration, base difficulty, per-role capability mapping, per-approach difficulty adjustment, police-pressure weight, bounded variance, and objective-outcome thresholds. Intelligence definitions specify relevant topics, maximum useful age, and maximum execution advantage. Runtime intelligence quality is derived from the best selected report per relevant topic using reliability, specificity, and freshness; missing topics contribute no knowledge and duplicate reports do not stack. Runtime resolution consumes assigned-role capability, leader Management capability, selected planning intelligence, modeled target police presence, deadline pressure, and state-owned RNG variance rather than a universal success percentage.
- Authorized operations whose scheduled time arrives begin through the deterministic top-level tick pipeline in stable ID order and are indexed by due resolution time rather than scanned every tick. Due operations resolve in stable order to `Achieved`, `Partial`, or `Failed` objective outcomes with persisted causal factors. A separate deterministic exposure calculation uses authored operation exposure, local police presence, participant Stealth capability, selected planning intelligence, approach, and its own state-owned RNG draw, so objective success and operational exposure are not the same result. Exposure persists as `None`, `Trace`, `Witnessed`, or `Identifying` with its causal factors. Completion creates organization-held `AfterAction` information and a linked campaign-history event, and the operation stores those durable record IDs rather than caching an isolated result string.
- Authority exceptions are durable decision records with requester, recipient, context, attention class, offered responses, timestamps, lifecycle, and version. An operation in `AwaitingDecision` has exactly one pending decision and can leave that state only through decision resolution. Waiting time is excluded from execution time: the operation records its pause point, leaves the due-work index while awaiting input, and shifts its resolution deadline by the exact paused duration if ordered to continue.
- Attention classes are shared simulation data (`Routine`, `Notable`, `Exception`, `Crisis`). Persistent campaign settings control which classes request adapter-level auto-pause behavior.
- Manager delegation is represented by persistent mandates with organization ownership, one active mandate per manager, geographic and functional responsibility scopes, standing policy overrides, optional budget authority, versioning, historical revocation, and synchronized indexes.
- Delegated authority has one canonical resolution path that validates mandate lifecycle, manager ownership and membership, responsibility scope, and versioned manager/mandate dependencies. Finance and routine enterprises consume the same authority model. Active enterprises prevent revoking their mandate or removing the scope they currently depend on; suspension releases that active dependency and resumption requires fresh authority.
- Manager policy resolution is deterministic: an active mandate standing order overrides the organization policy for that manager; otherwise the organization policy is inherited.
- Law-enforcement jurisdiction is persistent institutional state owned by specific law-enforcement or legal-authority organizations. Jurisdictions cover explicit neighborhoods, carry versioned case-intake priority, and have synchronized neighborhood-to-authority indexes. Incident routing chooses the highest-priority active authority deterministically with stable-ID tie breaking; a crime in a neighborhood with no assigned authority does not implicitly create a global police case. Validated operation-resolution tokens snapshot both the routing result, including the absence of an authority, and any chosen jurisdiction version so later jurisdiction changes cannot redirect a stale incident commit.
- Investigations and evidence are specific persistent records with legal-institution ownership, subjects, custody, causal origin, optional named source, evidence kind, support strength, source reliability, admissibility, explicit derived-evidence lineage, and synchronized case-graph indexes. Evidence can add newly discovered subjects to an existing investigation; reverse indexes support finding every case involving an entity, evidence by kind/origin/source, and every derived record that depends on earlier evidence. Foreign institutions cannot append evidence to another authority's case through the canonical evidence path. Active investigations also support explicit lead and supporting investigator assignments. Staffing requires active same-authority characters with Investigation capability, is indexed both by case and investigator, increments the investigation version, survives save/load, and blocks organization reassignment until the investigator is released. Assignment tokens snapshot both case and character versions so new evidence, staffing changes, investigative work, or personnel changes cannot be overwritten by stale commits.
- Investigation lifecycle is transactional rather than a passive status flag. Active cases can be suspended or closed; suspended cases can be resumed after their retained staffing is revalidated; closed cases are terminal. Scheduled detective work blocks suspension or closure instead of disappearing. Suspended and closed cases preserve staffing and history but no longer lock investigators to the owning organization. Referral remains intentionally unavailable through the generic lifecycle API because transferring or sharing a case between authorities requires a separate custody/provenance model.
- Case-graph inference resolves deterministic shortest evidence paths using only subject/origin links from evidence already held by that investigation. Stable entity and evidence ordering breaks equal-length ties. The query does not consult hidden social relationships, unrelated world truth, or evidence owned by another case, preserving the knowledge boundary while providing a reusable foundation for investigative reasoning.
- Detective pattern analysis is the first authored investigation-work type. It is scheduled against an assigned investigator and a canonical pair of entities, snapshots the case-owned evidence path available when work is authorized, and resolves after an authored six-hour duration through the normal deterministic tick pipeline. Resolution uses Investigation capability, persisted source-evidence support, path complexity, authored difficulty/variance, and state-owned RNG. Successful work creates a `PatternLink` with exact source-evidence provenance; weak work remains durably inconclusive; new direct evidence can supersede pending analysis without fabricating another link. Due work is indexed by `SimTime`, duplicate reverse focuses are prevented, stale case/personnel tokens are rejected, and save/load one minute before resolution produces identical outcomes.
- Named witnesses are persistent case records separate from anonymous testimony. A case witness links a concrete character to one investigation with mutable `Hostile`, `Reluctant`, or `Cooperative` state; individual statements persist immutable confidence, summary, subject/origin, time, and a one-to-one testimony evidence record. Named testimony stores the witness as evidence source while preserving causal origin separately, and confidence deterministically maps to evidence strength/reliability. Current witness activity requires an active case and active witness character, while earlier testimony remains valid through suspension and save/load. Anonymous incident testimony remains valid with no named source, so authored operation exposure does not invent a person when the simulation has not identified one.
- Operation exposure can atomically open a jurisdiction-owned investigation and attach its initial evidence when a resolvable neighborhood has an assigned authority. Operation-originated evidence records the operation as its causal origin, carries exposure-dependent strength/reliability, and may identify a concrete participant at the highest exposure level. The after-action report describes only crew-observable exposure and does not reveal the hidden investigation or evidence graph to the player without a separate intelligence path.
- Reports store player-facing summaries linked to known information, entities, and optional durable decision IDs. Persisted organization reports may cite organization-held information only; character knowledge must first be explicitly transferred into organization knowledge, preventing historical reports from becoming invalid when personnel later leave. Decision-linked reports remain valid after resolution because decision records are historical state.
- Campaign history stores durable entity-linked events for later timelines and campaign recall.
- Finance uses typed monetary accounts and a balanced double-entry-style ledger. Street cash, concealed cash, accounted funds, legitimate operating funds, receivables, payables, and settlement accounts are distinct states rather than one universal cash scalar.
- Multi-account financial transactions validate every posting and resulting balance before a consuming commit token atomically changes any account. Account versions prevent stale validated transactions from overwriting newer balances.
- Mandate budgets are tied to ledger history rather than duplicated spend counters. A budget specifies an organization-owned funding account, a limit, and a daily or weekly period. Delegated transactions explicitly identify the mandate manager and responsibility scope exercising authority, persist that authority snapshot with the mandate version, enforce scope and manager ownership before mutation, and reject stale mandate or manager snapshots.
- Routine criminal enterprises are persistent delegated records rather than repeated player commands. The current authored enterprise set contains protection and gambling. Establishment binds an organization, manager mandate, responsibility scope, location, cash account, and permanently exclusive settlement account; lifecycle supports active, suspended, and closed states with versioned consuming transitions.
- Enterprise economics are immutable registry definitions. Daily cycles derive gross revenue and operating cost from authored economics plus neighborhood illicit demand, commercial activity, wealth, police presence, manager Management capability, bounded state-owned RNG variance, and any relevant standing policy snapshot. Results persist gross, cost, net, variance, manager capability, policy, attention class, ledger transaction, and optional information provenance rather than collapsing to a universal profit scalar.
- Active enterprise work is indexed by next-cycle `SimTime` in deterministic order, so the minute pipeline processes due work without scanning every enterprise. Establishment and resume tokens carry authored cycle duration rather than a precomputed timestamp, anchoring the next cycle to actual commit time even when validation and commit are separated. Cycle settlement uses the normal balanced ledger transaction path and then advances enterprise history/schedule atomically.
- Enterprise performance participates in the attention model without inventing crises: normal variance is `Routine`; authored material-variance thresholds produce `Notable` cycles. A notable cycle creates precise `AfterAction` information held by the owning organization and linked to its manager and enterprise. Routine cycles remain compact financial history.
- Enterprise financial reporting is derived on demand from cycle history rather than cached totals. It supports drill-down by enterprise/racket, organization, manager, exact location, and neighborhood, including gross revenue, operating cost, net cash, cycle count, and notable-cycle count. Historical windows exclude enterprises not yet established by the window end. Generated enterprise financial reports contain a routine aggregate plus chronological notable entries whose sources are the organization's after-action information records.
- Legitimate businesses can run independent operating economies through a separate `EconomyState`. Each operating business owns distinct legitimate-operating and settlement accounts, a deterministic due-time schedule, versioned lifecycle, and durable cycle history. Establishment and resume schedules begin from actual commit time, and historical summaries exclude operating economies not yet established by the requested window end. Authored business economics consume neighborhood wealth and commercial activity; material variances produce owner-scoped accountant information rather than hidden truth. Organization financial reporting compares legitimate business cashflow with illicit enterprise cashflow from persisted cycle history.
- Business-hosted enterprises consume concrete venue functions from the business record. For example, authored gambling venues require cash handling, meeting space, and customer access; these requirements are checked at establishment, cycle planning, resume, and save/load against the immutable registry.
- Save envelopes are versioned, content-revision checked, structurally and registry validated, and preserve RNG continuation. The current authored content revision is 5.
- The current in-memory state schema version is 14. Typed information topics and their indexes, operation-selected intelligence, operation exposure factors/consequences, legal jurisdictions, investigator staffing, scheduled investigation work, named witnesses/statements, evidence source/derivation provenance, and their reverse indexes join operation schedules/resolution records, decision/delegation state, authority-bearing ledger history, neighborhood/business profiles, legitimate and illicit economy history, information lineage, and report source links inside the serialized deterministic continuation boundary.
- `validate_state` provides release-safe structural validation; debug builds additionally run `validate_invariants` at the top-level simulation boundary. Registry-aware validation recomputes authored operation intelligence effects, objective margins/outcomes, exposure arithmetic, authored exposure evidence kinds, investigation-work duration/factors/outcomes, and derived pattern evidence as well as business/enterprise requirements and attention thresholds. Runtime validation covers operation lifecycle chronology, due-work indexes, selected-intelligence ownership/relevance, after-action/history links, jurisdiction ownership, investigation lifecycle/staffing, detective-work source paths and chronology, witness/statement linkage, named evidence sources, derived evidence provenance, exposure-to-investigation/evidence linkage, economy/enterprise authority, location and account roles, cycle arithmetic, ledger linkage, owner-scoped information, and acyclic information provenance.
- The current suite has 85 passing unit tests plus doc tests. A deterministic mixed-state soak test covers scheduled operation execution through jurisdictional evidence creation, explicit member-to-organization intelligence transfer, pending-decision reporting, authority-linked ledger spending, remaining budget capacity, three legitimate business cycles, three delegated enterprise cycles, and combined organization financial reporting over 5,000 ticks. Focused legal coverage includes jurisdiction routing/staleness, versioned investigator staffing, suspend/resume/terminal close, scheduled-work transition blocking, evidence-only shortest paths, successful/inconclusive/superseded pattern analysis, exact derived-evidence lineage, deterministic save/load immediately before detective-work resolution, fresh investigation-work/witness/statement/evidence ID allocation after restore, named witness registration/cooperation staleness, confidence-derived testimony, source indexes, anonymous-testimony compatibility, and testimony persistence through case suspension.

## Architectural Boundaries

- Core systems contain no file, network, database, UI, or process IO.
- Registries contain definitions only; generated campaign values remain in `AppState` or owned records.
- Indexes are derived state and are private to the owner that keeps them synchronized.
- Consequential cross-record mutation uses validation before commit. Multi-resource transactions use consuming validated tokens. Tokens that depend on mutable records capture record versions and reject stale commits; dependencies that can change independently of the primary record are revalidated at commit.
- Player knowledge is represented separately from world truth. Reports reference information records rather than reading hidden state as if it were known.
- There is no universal heat value. Legal pressure is represented by actual investigations and evidence.
- There is no tactical movement or combat-control layer in the core architecture.
- Core predicate naming follows `is_`, `has_`, and `can_`; synchronized-index validation uses `has_consistent_indexes` project-wide.

## Intentionally Not Yet Modeled

The foundation deliberately does not fabricate shallow implementations for systems whose data model needs further design. Major domains still to be introduced through their own records, registries, systems, indexes, invariants, persistence coverage, and behavioral tests include:

- City geography beyond neighborhood economic/institutional profiles: transport topology, schedules, patrol deployment, population, and richer institution networks.
- Employment, wages, supply chains, business ownership transfer/history, laundering plausibility, asset quantities/condition, market competition, and broader citywide supply/demand. Legitimate business operating cycles and categorical asset functions now exist, but they intentionally do not fabricate those deeper economic systems. Protection and gambling prove the routine illicit-enterprise foundation; bookmaking, loan sharking, alcohol distribution, fencing, labor racketeering, fraud, and other authored enterprise types are not yet modeled.
- Delegated staffing selection, responsibility handoff, personnel requirements, dependency resolution, and richer manager behavior beyond the current mandate-driven routine enterprise cycles.
- Character needs, family ties, secrets, injuries, legal status, recruitment networks, and behavior decision systems.
- Operation equipment and reusable vehicles, injuries/casualties, damaged or recovered assets, explicit bad-intelligence misinformation effects, patrol schedules/response timing, and richer condition-specific exposure. Scheduled completion now has separate causal objective and exposure outcomes plus jurisdictional evidence consequences, but interrupted/aborted operations do not yet have the same detailed consequence model.
- Broader autonomous detective lead generation, selection/prioritization among possible investigative work, case merging, institutional investigative resource budgets, witness intimidation/protection and availability over time, referrals/evidence sharing between authorities, arrest, charging, bail, prosecution, courts, lawyers, informants, corruption, politics, press, labor institutions, and rival strategy. One explicit evidence-driven `PatternAnalysis` work type and named witness testimony now exist, but they intentionally do not fabricate unseen links or generic autonomous police behavior.
- Generated opportunities, executive-brief synthesis, campaign pressures, historical transitions, and end-state evaluation.

These omissions are intentional. New systems should be added only when their canonical ownership and transaction boundaries are clear enough to preserve the design law.
